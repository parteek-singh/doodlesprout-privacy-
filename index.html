DoodleSprout

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

The short version

  • DoodleSprout is a colouring and learning app for young children (about 2–6 years old).
  • No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no analytics, no chat.
  • Your child's name, drawings and progress are stored only on your device. We never receive them.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat only to check whether the Creative Pass is active, using an anonymous ID.
  • Purchases, settings and links that leave the app are behind a grown-ups-only parental gate.

1. Who we are

DoodleSprout ("we", "us", "our") is an independent app made by Parteek Singh Slathia. If you have any question about this policy or your child's data, email slathia.parteeksingh@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, DoodleSprout saves the following locally on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we have no servers that store it.

WhatWhy
A first name or nickname for your child (optional — you can skip it)To say "Hi" on screen and out loud
Age range (2–4 or 4–6)To choose suitable brushes and activities
Drawings and colouring progressSo your child can continue and see their gallery
Stars, stickers, pictures finished, letters and numbers practisedRewards for your child and the progress view in the Parent Dashboard
Time spent in the app todayThe optional daily time limit and the Parent Dashboard
Settings (sound, music, daily time limit)To remember your choices

Spoken words (such as world and picture names) are produced by your device's built-in text-to-speech; nothing is recorded or sent. DoodleSprout does not use the camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos library. Printing a drawing sends it from your device to the printer you choose through iOS; it is not sent to us.

Deleting it: use Parent Dashboard → Reset progress, or delete the app. Either removes this information from your device.

3. Purchases (Creative Pass)

The Creative Pass is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, email, card or other payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

To check whether the Creative Pass is active (and to restore it on another device), the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives:

  • a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app (not linked to your or your child's name);
  • purchase information such as the product, price, currency, date and store receipt;
  • basic technical information such as device model, operating system and app version, and country or region.

This is used only to provide and verify purchases. It is not used for advertising or tracking. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

4. What we don't do

  • No advertising of any kind, and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or profiling, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No accounts, logins, chat, social features or sharing with other users.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information.

5. Children's privacy

DoodleSprout is made for children, so we have designed it to collect as little as possible. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children: anything your child enters (such as their name) and everything they draw stays on your device. This approach is intended to meet children's privacy laws including the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR, and the Australian Privacy Act.

Grown-ups-only areas — purchases, the Parent Dashboard, and any link or email that leaves the app — are protected by a parental gate that asks a question young children can't easily answer.

6. Contacting us from the app

If a parent chooses Contact us in the Parent Dashboard, your email app opens and you decide what to send. We receive your email address and message, use them only to reply, and delete them on request.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete all on-device data at any time with Reset progress or by deleting the app.
  • Ask us to delete any email you've sent us, or to request deletion of purchase records held by RevenueCat for your anonymous ID — email us and we'll help.
  • Manage or cancel a subscription in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

8. Data retention and security

On-device data stays until you delete it. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are kept as needed to provide purchases and meet legal and tax obligations. We rely on your device's built-in security and on Apple's and RevenueCat's safeguards for purchase data.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how DoodleSprout handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a change would mean collecting new information from children, we will ask for a parent's consent first.
