How can we help?

A grown-up can contact us at slathia.parteeksingh@gmail.com. Tell us your iPad model, iPadOS version, and what happened. Please avoid sending your child’s personal information or payment details.

Restore Creative Pass

Use the same Apple Account used for the original purchase. Connect to the internet, open the grown-ups area, pass the parental gate, then select Restore purchases. Your artwork is stored on the device and is not transferred by restoring purchases.

Manage or cancel a subscription

Open iPad Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or manage Apple subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Lifetime purchases do not renew.

Keep your artwork safe

Artwork and progress are stored locally on your device. Reset progress or deleting the app removes that local data. Avoid deleting the app as a troubleshooting step if you want to keep your drawings.

Sound and daily limits

The Parent Dashboard contains voice, music and daily time limit settings. Access it through the grown-ups button and parental gate.
